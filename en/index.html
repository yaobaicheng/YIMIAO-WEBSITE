Hailiang Copper Tubes

Hailiang Co., Ltd. is a leading global copper tube processor and has ranked No. 1 worldwide in copper tube shipment volume for six consecutive years. It supplies trusted copper tube products to the air-conditioning, refrigeration and HVAC industries. YIMIAO HUANYU brings Hailiang’s quality products and brand value to more customers in Indonesia. We provide ready stock, product selection support and purchasing services—so customers can buy with confidence and use products with peace of mind.
